# WordReview Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

This policy explains how MobileSoft's WordReview app for iPhone and iPad handles information.

[Support](index.html)

## Information handled by the app

WordReview stores the information needed for vocabulary practice locally on your device. This includes:

- Word lists you create, edit or import.
- Incorrect answers saved when you complete a practice session.
- Practice dates, exercise types, languages, questions and answers associated with saved mistakes.

WordReview does not require an account. We do not collect or receive your word lists, practice answers or mistake history through the app.

## Advertising and analytics

WordReview does not include advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

We do not use the app to track your activity across other companies' apps or websites.

## Speech synthesis

WordReview uses Apple's system speech synthesis to read exercise text aloud.

The app does not record your voice or use the microphone for dictation practice. You enter answers by typing.

System voices and related operating-system services are provided by Apple.

## Files and backups

When you choose a CSV file, WordReview accesses it to load or import your exercises.

If you choose a file stored with iCloud Drive or another file provider, that provider handles access according to its own policies and your settings.

WordReview's local files may be included in device backups, depending on your Apple settings. Backup storage and restoration are managed by the operating system and the backup service you use.

## Managing and deleting your information

You control the app's local data.

- Edit word lists and remove entries through the app's word-list editor.
- Delete selected mistakes through **Review Wrong Answers**.
- Manage word-list files and the `Data/Wrong.csv` file through the Files app.
- Delete WordReview from your device to remove its local app data.

Deleting local data does not automatically remove copies you exported, files stored with another provider or existing device backups.

## Support communications

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, such as a description of a problem or screenshots.

We use this information to respond to your request and investigate app issues. Email is handled through the email services used to send and receive the correspondence.

You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable record-retention obligations.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or information-handling practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.
